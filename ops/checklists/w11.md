# Checklist W11: Email drafts, inbox actions and hardware checks - Wally 1.10.0

Run it on BOTH apps: the phone app and the installed laptop app (`Wally-1.10.0.msi`). Do the setup first: the "Adding send and label access (Wally 1.10.0)" part of `ops/google-setup.md` (without it, sending fails with "Google permission missing"). The drafting and explaining parts need the XPS on with Ollama running (see `ops/ollama-xps.md`). Use the Settings `Sync now` button whenever a step says Sync. Tick a line only when it really passed; write "skipped" next to a line you did not run. A line marked "skipped" does NOT count as passed.

Use only your own second email address as the receiver (never a real contact), and only made-up test text.

Release (do this first):
- [ ] The server was deployed BEFORE the apps were installed (`powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ops\deploy-server.ps1`).
- [ ] On the laptop: Wally was quit from the tray icon (right-click > Quit) BEFORE installing the new MSI.
- [ ] Both apps show version 1.10.0.
- [ ] After the upgrade, the sidebar (laptop) and the More menu (phone) show `Mail` and `Hardware` after `Screentime`.
- [ ] Entries saved before the upgrade (check-ins, journal, spending, meals, docs, dreams, goals, tasks, study files, exams, listens) are all still listed on the laptop and on the phone.
- [ ] `ssh -t hp sudo journalctl -u assistant -n 50 --no-pager` shows a recent `google poll ok` line and Browse > Mail still shows `Unread mail` (reading mail still works after the new sign-in).

Backup and restore (new tables):
- [ ] The restore helper on the HP was refreshed (`ops/backup-setup.md`, "Update the restore helper after a schema change"), a fresh backup was made, and the restore test prints `mail_draft`, `mail_action` and `hw_check` and `PASS`.

Reply to a mail (SPEC section 2 #18):
- [ ] Send yourself a mail from your second address, with a subject like `Wally test 1`. After `Sync now` it shows in the Mail tab's unread list.
- [ ] Tap `Draft reply` on it, type a short instruction (for example "say thanks"). After a minute (the laptop must be open) a draft appears. The To is your second address and the Subject is `Re: Wally test 1`. The draft says "Draft". Nothing arrived in your second address's inbox yet.
- [ ] Open the draft, edit one word in the body, `Save`. The edited word is still there after `Sync now` on the other device.
- [ ] Press `Send`. Within a minute the draft shows "Sent" on both devices. The mail arrives in your second address, in the same conversation as `Wally test 1` (the thread view shows both), and it is in Gmail's Sent folder.
- [ ] Open the "Sent" draft: you cannot edit it. `Delete` removes it from the list on both devices.
- [ ] Make another draft reply and change the Subject to something different before `Send`. The editor says "Changed subject: sends as a new conversation", and the mail arrives as a separate conversation.
- [ ] Make a draft with a Hebrew subject and body, `Send` it to your second address. It arrives with the Hebrew text correct, not question marks.
- [ ] Make a draft and do NOT press `Send`. Wait 20 minutes with the laptop open: it still says "Draft" and nothing was sent.
- [ ] Make a draft with an empty or wrong To address (for example `abc`): `Send` is disabled.

New email:
- [ ] `New email`: the To is empty. Type your second address, a subject and a short instruction. The AI writes the body and the subject you can change. `Send` works as above.

Organise inbox (SPEC section 2 #20):
- [ ] Make sure your second address has sent you at least two unread mails (for example "Newsletter test" and "Invoice test"), and that your Gmail already has at least one label of your own.
- [ ] Press `Organise inbox`. After a minute, suggestions appear. Every suggested label is one of your existing Gmail labels (no new label name appears). Nothing changed in Gmail yet.
- [ ] `Reject` one suggestion: it disappears from the open list, and Gmail is unchanged for that mail.
- [ ] `Approve` one `Label` suggestion: within a minute it shows as done, and in Gmail the mail has that label.
- [ ] `Approve` one `Archive` suggestion: within a minute it shows as done, the mail is out of the Gmail inbox (still found under All Mail), and it disappears from the unread list in Browse > Mail.
- [ ] Handled suggestions (done or rejected) can be deleted from the list.

Hardware (SPEC section 2 #21, #22; the XPS):
- [ ] On the laptop, `Hardware` tab, press `Run check`. After a few seconds a check appears with the time and a one-line summary. Opening it shows the Computer, Battery, Disks and Recent errors sections with real values (your battery capacity and cycle count, your disks' health).
- [ ] If any section cannot be read, it says "Not available" and the other sections are still filled in (if all sections work, write "n/a" and do not tick this line).
- [ ] The phone has no `Run check` button. After `Sync now` on the phone, the check from the laptop is listed there and opens with the same values.
- [ ] Type a problem (for example "the battery drains fast") in `Explain` on the laptop. After a minute an answer appears with likely causes and next steps, and it mentions values from the check (for example the battery capacity numbers).
- [ ] On the phone, open the check, type a problem and press `Explain`. It says it is queued; once the laptop is open and has synced, the answer appears on the phone after `Sync now`.
- [ ] `Delete` a check: it is gone from both devices.

Safety checks:
- [ ] A mail you never approved was never sent: Gmail's Sent folder holds only the mails you pressed `Send` for during this checklist.
- [ ] Wally Calendar's "Make Wally task" button still works (it uses a separate key that cannot touch email, see `ops/wallycal.md`).
- [ ] `ssh -t hp sudo journalctl -u assistant -n 100 --no-pager` shows no password, token or mail text.
