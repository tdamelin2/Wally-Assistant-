# Checklist W12: Chat tools, approvals and Codex research - Wally 1.12.0

Run it on BOTH apps: the phone app and the installed laptop app (`Wally-1.12.0.msi`). Do the setup first: Codex must be installed and signed in on the laptop (see `ops/codex.md`), the XPS must be on with Ollama running (see `ops/ollama-xps.md`), and the HP server must be updated. Use the Settings `Sync now` button whenever a step says Sync. Tick a line only when it really passed; write "skipped" next to a line you did not run. A line marked "skipped" does NOT count as passed. Use only made-up test text.

Release (do this first):
- [ ] The server was deployed BEFORE the apps were installed (`powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ops\deploy-server.ps1`).
- [ ] On the laptop: Wally was quit from the tray icon (right-click > Quit) BEFORE installing the new MSI.
- [ ] Both apps show version 1.12.0.
- [ ] There is no `Approvals` tab any more. The sidebar (laptop) and the More menu (phone) have `Files`, and Files has an `Approvals` section (pending cards first, then history, then the Research list).
- [ ] Entries saved before the upgrade (check-ins, journal, spending, meals, docs, dreams, goals, tasks, study files, exams, listens, mail drafts) are all still listed on the laptop and on the phone.

Chat reads your data (SPEC section 2 #23):
- [ ] In Chat ask: "what did I do on my laptop yesterday?". Under the answer you see "Looked at: ..." and the answer contains real values (apps and minutes) from your Screentime, not invented ones.
- [ ] Ask for something you have no data for (a day with nothing recorded). Chat says it has nothing, it does not make numbers up.

Approvals (SPEC section 2 #24):
- [ ] In Chat ask: "make a task to email my lecturer". A card appears with the task text and the buttons Approve and Reject. No new task exists yet (check Tasks).
- [ ] Tap Reject on one card: the card turns to Rejected and no task is made.
- [ ] Ask again and tap Approve: exactly one task is made, and the card turns to Done.
- [ ] Ask for a third card and leave it Pending. After `Sync now` on the other device the same card is there too (in the chat and in Files > Approvals). It shows how long it has left (it expires after 7 days).
- [ ] Ask Chat to send an email or archive a mail: you only get an approval card, and after Approve only a draft (Mail tab, DRAFT) or a suggestion appears. Nothing is sent or archived by itself.

Approve in the chat and add things by chat (Wally 1.12.0, Tim change):
- [ ] Approval cards can be approved right in the chat (Approve and Reject buttons on the card), on the phone and on the laptop. Approving there does the same as before: one result only.
- [ ] Ask: "add a meal: two eggs and toast". A card shows the meal. Nothing is in Log > Meals until you tap Approve; after Approve the meal is there once.
- [ ] Ask: "add spending 30 on lunch today". Same: card first, then one spending entry after Approve.
- [ ] Ask Chat to add a check-in (give mood and the other values it asks for) and a journal entry. Each gives a card first and one entry after Approve.
- [ ] Make a task, then ask: "change the task <its name> to be due on Friday" (or "mark it done"). A card shows the old and the new value. The task is unchanged until Approve; after Approve it has the new value. Approving the same card twice changes it only once.
- [ ] Ask: "make a study guide for <a course that has a study set>". A card appears; after Approve and a short wait (the XPS must be on) a doc "Study guide: <course name>" is in Files > Study guides. Ask again and Approve: the same doc is updated, there is still only one.
- [ ] Ask for something with a missing detail, for example "put a dentist appointment in my calendar tomorrow at 15:00" (no length). Wally asks how long it lasts and does not guess. Answer "one hour" and then a card appears.
- [ ] Chat has no way to delete anything and never sends mail. Ask "delete my task <name>": nothing is deleted (Wally says it cannot).

Chat history:
- [ ] Chat history stays: close and reopen Wally (laptop) or the app (phone). The earlier messages and cards are still there, and cards still show their current state (Pending, Done or Rejected).
- [ ] The history is not shared: what you chat on the phone does not appear on the laptop.
- [ ] Press `Clear` in the chat. A question asks you to confirm. After confirming the chat is empty, and still empty after restarting the app. Pressing it and then cancelling the question keeps the messages.

Research through Codex (SPEC section 2 #27 and #28):
- [ ] In Chat ask: "how many calories are in hummus?". After a short wait (Codex takes about 20 seconds) the answer appears in the chat with source links, and Files > Approvals > Research list has a "hummus" row. No approval card was needed. Food calories are always researched, never guessed by Wally from memory.
- [ ] On the laptop, while that research runs, the queued text says Wally is looking it up with Codex and the answer will appear here in about 20 seconds. It never says "open Wally on the laptop". On the phone the text may say the answer comes when Wally is open on the laptop.
- [ ] Ask the same question again. The answer comes back at once from the Research list, and the laptop did not call Codex again (no new "Working" line).
- [ ] Ask a well-known explanation, for example "why is the sky black at night?". Wally answers it directly, with no card and no Codex.
- [ ] Ask a niche fact Wally would have to guess (for example "what is the population of a small village I name" or a price). An "Ask Codex" approval card shows the exact question text. Nothing is sent to Codex before you tap Approve. After Approve the answer appears (laptop open), and the row shows in the Research list.
- [ ] Ask about an item with an email address or a long number in its name ("calories in me@example.com"). It does not run on its own: you get a card with the exact text instead.
- [ ] Delete a row from the Research list. Asking the same question again researches it again.

Meal Look up (SPEC section 2 #29):
- [ ] In Log > Meals add a meal "hummus and a banana", then tap `Look up` on its row. The row shows "Queued" then "Working", and when done shows "≈ ... kcal (research)" with protein, carbs and fat replaced by the researched values.
- [ ] Add another meal that uses the same foods and tap `Look up`. It finishes quickly because the foods are already in the Research list (no Codex wait).
- [ ] While a Look up is running, the button on that meal is disabled.
- [ ] Edit the meal text while its Look up is still running. The result is not written over your edit and the row says to look it up again.

When Codex fails (SPEC section 2 #30):
- [ ] (Since Phase 17 research runs on the HP server, not the laptop: rename the Codex program on the HP or sign it out, instead of quitting Wally.) Quit Wally, rename the Codex program for a moment (or sign out of Codex), start Wally and ask a research question. The research shows "Failed: Codex is not set up on the server" and everything else (Chat, tasks, Sync) keeps working. Put Codex back and ask again: it works.
- [ ] Only if it happens: when your ChatGPT allowance is used up, the research shows "Codex limit reached", Chat and the Research list say "Codex is out of usage. Research and Look up will work again when your ChatGPT allowance resets.", and a meal's Look up status says "Codex is out of usage - try again later". Nothing else breaks. (Skip this line if you never run out; do not try to use up the allowance on purpose.)

Two devices and errors:
- [ ] Approve the same card on both devices at nearly the same time. Only one result exists (one task, not two).
- [ ] Stop the HP server: Chat still opens, Files > Approvals still lists saved cards, Sync shows a short error and nothing hangs.
