Interview 2026-11-12 (Tim + Claude), after the Phase 17 review. Research: `RESEARCH.md` (same folder).
Project: `C:\Users\user\Projects\assistant`. Decision ids use the prefix B (B1–B35).
The plan went through four independent reviews on 2026-11-12 (66, 73, 80, 78 out of 100). Most blocking findings came from the learning feature, so Tim moved it to a later phase (B35); B27 and B31 are kept below as the starting point for that phase. A fifth review of the smaller plan (83) found that the sorter could still save a look-alike sentence, so the sorter was made read-only (B33). A sixth (86) found a router rule that was too loose ("I had … for dinner"), so the add rules were narrowed to explicit command forms and tested against look-alike sentences. A seventh (89) found one more of the same kind ("add lunch with Dana at 1" is a calendar plan, not a meal); it and that review's 17 small notes were fixed without a further review.
